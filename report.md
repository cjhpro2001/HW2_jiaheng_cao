# Report

## Step 2: Business Workflow and Task Definition

### Workflow Chosen
The workflow I chose is generating investment-style business summaries from raw company information or news.

### Target User
The target users are financial analysts, investment professionals, or business consultants who need to quickly understand a company’s performance and key insights.

### Input to the System
The system takes unstructured or semi-structured text as input, such as:
- company news articles  
- financial performance descriptions  
- short business reports  

### Expected Output
The system generates a clear and professional business summary that includes:
- key performance highlights  
- main drivers of growth or decline  
- potential risks or concerns  
- an overall analytical tone suitable for business use  

### Why This Task is Valuable
This task is valuable because professionals often need to process large amounts of information quickly. Automating this workflow can save time, improve consistency, and provide a strong first draft that can be further refined by analysts.
